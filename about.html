About Us

We are Garden Bloom Flowers!
Our journey started in our home garage in 2022, selling to our community on special occasions.
Our business was made out of passion and love for roses , and taking care of them.
Our Bloemfontein community members made us what we are today!

Their love and support blossmed our small business into what it is today!

Our Mission

To bring smiles and create beautiful memorable floral memories through our elegant flower arrangements.

Our Vision

To become Bloemfontein and surrounding areas' go-to flower market for floral experiences and flower arrangements.

Our Team

Currently we are a team of 3, consisting of me, the owner, my husband and our son.
